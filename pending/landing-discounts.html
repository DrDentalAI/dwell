How it works

Answer four questions. Get a ranked list.

Your vehicle, where you charge, which networks you already have accounts with, and whether you drive for a gig platform. That's the whole setup. Everything after that is arithmetic — and it's arithmetic nobody is doing for you today.

The app watches for real promo codes too. It just doesn't pretend they're the main event.

Eligibility, not a coupon list

Rideshare enrollments, automaker discounts, subscriptions, card-linked offers and loyalty programs — ranked by annual dollars at your actual usage.

Catches the bad trade

If a paid plan you're on is worth less than a free enrollment you qualify for, the app says so on the first screen. That happens more often than it should.

Crosses the border

Your EVgo membership is worth exactly nothing in Ontario — EVgo has no Canadian stations. Switch regions and the whole list changes.

The honest part

It can't type the code into your EVgo app. Nothing can.

No charging network publishes an interface that accepts a promo code from outside its own app, and both app stores forbid one app driving another app's screen. Android tightened that further in March 2026 — the workaround everyone reaches for now breaks on any phone with Advanced Protection turned on.

So ChargeStack does the thing that actually works and never breaks: one tap copies the code and opens the network app. You paste. No permissions to grant, nothing to go wrong on the next OS update.

Anyone promising you auto-redemption is either about to get pulled from the store or hasn't shipped yet.
